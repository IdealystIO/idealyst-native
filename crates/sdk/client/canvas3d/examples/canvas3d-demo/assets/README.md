# canvas3d-demo assets

`WaterBottle.glb` and `Fox.glb` are fetched, not committed. Run:

    ./scripts/fetch-canvas3d-demo-model.sh

Both come from the Khronos
[glTF-Sample-Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets)
repository, at the revision pinned in the fetch script.

**Water Bottle**
([source](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/WaterBottle))
is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

**Fox** ([source](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox)):

- model by PixelMannen (2014), [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/);
- rigging and animation by tomkranis (2014),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
- conversion to glTF by @AsoboStudio and @scurest (2017),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The demo shows the CC BY credit in its HUD while the Fox is on screen.
